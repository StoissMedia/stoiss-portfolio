FAQ

Good to know

How do revisions work?

Every edit includes two revision rounds, and changes and small additions both count. Send your feedback in one pass per round. Extra rounds are $40, and a brand-new segment or change of direction is quoted separately.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes, gladly, before you share any material. Most of my work is under NDA, and I never show or share confidential client work, publicly or privately. Your project stays yours.

How do I send footage or scripts?

Any file-sharing link works: Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or similar. For scripts, a doc or PDF is perfect.

How does payment work?

Direct clients are invoiced in USD, with a 50% deposit for new clients. If you prefer, you can also hire me through Upwork, Fiverr or Voices.com.

Do you take rush jobs?

Often, yes, depending on my schedule. Mention your deadline in the brief and I’ll tell you straight away if it’s possible.

What can you translate?

Pretty much anything, in both directions, English ↔ Danish: legal documents, commercial and marketing copy, UGC and social content, and books, fiction and nonfiction alike. Games are a specialty, with in-game LQA on shipped titles, but they’re one part of the work, not the limit.
