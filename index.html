FAQ

Good to know

How do revisions work?

Every quote includes a round of revisions. Feedback is collected in one pass so changes are quick and nothing gets missed. Larger changes to the brief are quoted separately.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Most of my work is under NDA, and I’m happy to sign yours before you share any material.

How do I send footage or scripts?

Any file-sharing link works: Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or similar. For scripts, a doc or PDF is perfect.

How does payment work?

Direct clients are invoiced in EUR or DKK. If you prefer, you can also hire me through Upwork, Fiverr or Voices.com.

Do you take rush jobs?

Often, yes, depending on my schedule. Mention your deadline in the brief and I’ll tell you straight away if it’s possible.

Can you translate from Danish to English too?

Yes. I translate in both directions. Game localization is mostly English → Danish, but I take Danish → English work as well.

About

I’ve spent more than a decade behind the scenes of online content: editing for YouTube creators, working in live streaming, and running social media. Stoiss Media brings that experience under one name.
