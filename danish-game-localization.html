What I do

Text that works in the game.

A translation can be correct on paper and still break on screen. Testing it in the build is where most problems get caught.

  • Game translation

    UI, dialogue, tutorials, item text and achievements, translated so they read as if they were written in Danish.

  • In-build LQA

    Playing the build to catch cut-off text, wrong context, broken variables and lines that don’t fit the screen.

  • Proofreading existing Danish

    A second pass on a translation you already have, checked against the English source.

  • Store page & community

    Steam store text, patch notes and community announcements, in the same voice as the game.

  • Ongoing updates

    New content and patches translated as they ship, with terminology kept consistent across every update. That’s how I work on Dirge as a permanent team member. See credits ↓
