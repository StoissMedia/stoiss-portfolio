How it works

Clear rules mean a fixed price really stays fixed, for both of us.

  1. 1

    One shared Drive folder

    I set up a Google Drive folder for your project and you drop the raw files in. Full VODs are welcome.

  2. 2

    Timestamps: required and free

    A start and end time for the part you want is all I need. I clip it out myself.

  3. 3

    No timestamps? Sourcing add-on

    If I search a VOD to find the moment, a flat $25 (VODs over 4 h: $40).

  4. 4

    Files not quite right? Just re-send

    If a file arrives corrupted or below standard, I’ll let you know and you simply re-send it. No charge.

  5. 5

    Two revision rounds included

    Changes and small additions both count. Send your feedback in one pass per round. Extra rounds are $40 each.

  6. 6

    Brief locks when editing begins

    Tweaks and additions are handled inside your two revision rounds (or $40 for an extra round). A brand-new segment or a change of direction is quoted separately.

  7. 7

    Music & assets

    Background music is covered at no extra cost (YouTube’s free audio library). Custom thumbnails are available as an add-on, from $25.

  8. 8

    50% deposit for new clients

    Work starts when the deposit lands. Monthly packages are billed at the start of each month.

  9. 9

    Watermarked review copies

    You review a watermarked draft and use your revision rounds on it. Once the balance is paid in full, the clean final files are released. Prepaid monthly packages arrive clean.

  10. 10

    Fixed, transparent prices

    If the budget is tight, we adjust the scope (a different style, a shorter cut, a longer turnaround) so the price still fits.

  11. 11

    Longer projects, tailored quote

    Videos over 40 minutes, documentaries and long breakdowns get a custom quote, so the price matches the project.
